Kingdom Eco-Walks

Holy Stewardship Seminars

Educational workshops focused on sustainable waste management practices and broader environmental sensitization within local congregations.

Pilot Phase Timeline

Phase 1: Mobilization

Initial community outreach, identifying partner churches, and organizing the first Kingdom Eco-Walks.

Phase 2: Education

Conducting Holy Stewardship Seminars focusing on sustainable practices and theological foundations of ecology.

Phase 3: Action

Launching The Sacred Canopy tree planting events across designated church lands.
