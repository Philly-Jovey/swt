Our Identity

Creation Stewards-Uganda is a non-governmental organization rooted in the profound belief that caring for the earth is an act of worship and responsibility. Founded by a coalition of community leaders and environmental professionals, we bridge the gap between grassroots passion and sustainable ecological practices. Our identity is forged in the rich, vibrant landscapes of Uganda, driven by a commitment to protect biodiversity and empower local communities to become guardians of their natural heritage.

This mandate calls us beyond mere conservation; it is an active, joyful stewardship of the gifts we have been entrusted with.

Our Vision

To see communities and ecosystems thriving in harmony. We envision restored landscapes across Uganda where forests flourish, soils are healed, and communities are uplifted through sustainable livelihoods, reflecting the beauty and abundance of a carefully stewarded creation.

rocket_launch

Our Mission

To mobilize, equip, and empower faith communities and local leaders across Uganda to implement practical, scientifically sound environmental conservation projects. We strive to foster a culture of active stewardship through education, tree planting, and sustainable agriculture.
