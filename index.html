Our Calling

The Stewardship Mandate

park

Genesis 2:15

"The Lord God took the man and put him in the Garden of Eden to work it and take care of it."

At Creation Stewards-Uganda, we view environmental conservation not just as a civic duty, but as a divine mandate. We are called to actively protect, nurture, and restore the natural resources entrusted to us, fostering a sustainable future where both humanity and creation flourish together in harmony.

Our Approach

Core Pillars

Reforestation

The Sacred Canopy

Our flagship tree-planting initiative focuses on restoring degraded lands and creating robust green spaces. By treating the forest canopy as sacred, we combat deforestation and provide sustainable resources for local communities.

Eco-Tourism

Kingdom Eco-Walks

Immersive nature experiences designed to reconnect individuals with the beauty of creation. These guided walks educate participants on local biodiversity and inspire a deeper appreciation for the environment we are called to protect.

Education

Holy Stewardship Seminars

Educational workshops that bridge theology and ecology. We equip church leaders, youth, and community members with the knowledge and practical skills needed to implement sustainable environmental practices in their daily lives.
